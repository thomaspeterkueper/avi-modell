# Multimodal astronomical reconstruction
Status: methodological case study, not evidence for AVI.
Source: Chandra Treble Clef galaxy-cluster release, 2026-10-14, https://www.chandra.harvard.edu/photo/2026/trebleclef/
Separate measured radio, X-ray and infrared signals from derived structure and reconstructed physical history. The proposed sequence of black-hole activity and later cluster collision is inferred, not directly recorded. Next: verify the refereed paper, uncertainty model and competing explanations. Use as a provenance/uncertainty case study, not confirmation of speculative AVI physics.
